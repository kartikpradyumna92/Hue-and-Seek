Hue & Seek

Privacy Policy — effective June 7, 2026

Hue & Seek is a daily color-walk app made by Karteek Pradyumna Bulusu. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what leaves your device.

Short version: all your data stays on your device. Hue & Seek does not operate any servers, does not collect analytics, and does not share anything with third parties.

What the app accesses

UsedCamera

The camera is used solely to capture your daily color-walk photo. Photos are saved to your device's private app storage and optionally copied to your device gallery (Pictures/ColorWalk). No images are transmitted anywhere.

OptionalPrecise location (GPS)

Location is requested only at the moment you capture a photo. It is used to:

  • Reverse-geocode a readable label (e.g. "Sunnyvale, California") stored with the photo.
  • Embed GPS EXIF tags in the gallery copy so Google Photos can display the location.

Location data is stored locally in the app's database. It is never sent to any server or shared with any third party. You can deny location permission and the app will work normally without it; photos will simply have no location label.

OptionalPhoto library (read)

Read access to your photo library is used to:

  • Let you import a photo from your gallery instead of taking one in-app.
  • Recover your streak and photo history after a reinstall by scanning for ColorWalk_* files you previously saved.

The app only reads files whose name begins with ColorWalk_. It does not access or read any other photos on your device.

OptionalNotifications

If you grant notification permission, the app can send up to two daily reminders (morning and evening) to complete your color walk. Reminders are sent only if you have not already captured a photo that day. All notification scheduling happens locally on your device using Android's AlarmManager.

What the app does not do

  • Never Transmit photos, location, or any personal data to a server.
  • Never Collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
  • Never Use advertising SDKs or sell data to any third party.
  • Never Access contacts, microphone, calendar, or any other sensor.
  • Never Require an account or any form of sign-in.

Data storage and retention

All data (photo metadata, streak count, settings) is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database and SharedPreferences. It is cleared when you uninstall the app. The app supports Android Auto Backup so your settings and streak can be restored after a reinstall, using your existing Google account's encrypted backup — the same mechanism Android uses for all apps.

Children's privacy

Hue & Seek is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information in any form, so no special handling is required.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change materially, this page will be updated and the effective date will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Data deletion

All data Hue & Seek stores — photos, location metadata, streak count, and settings — lives exclusively on your device. You can delete it in two ways:

  • Individual photos: open the gallery, tap the delete icon on any photo. The photo and its associated location metadata are removed immediately.
  • All data at once: uninstall the app. Android automatically erases the app's private storage and database on uninstall. Any photos you previously copied to your device gallery (Pictures/ColorWalk) remain there and can be deleted manually from your gallery app.

To request assistance with data deletion, email kartikpradyumna@gmail.com.
